NITM Guide

Practical guide for team managers: sign in, enter a match result manually, and upload a Hyperscore JSON file.

Open NITM website Back to NITM info
Step 1 Sign in as team manager.
Step 2 Find the correct match and fill in the result.
Step 3 Or upload a JSON file exported from Hyperscore.

1. Sign in

  • Open the NITM website and sign in.
  • Use your team manager account.
  • Then choose the correct division and match.

2. Manual result entry

  • Open the match details.
  • Enter score and required fields.
  • Review and confirm the result.

3. Hyperscore import

  • Export the match in Hyperscore as JSON.
  • Use the upload option in NITM for that match.
  • Validate the imported data and save.

No access or upload issue?

Contact us and include team, division, and match date so we can help quickly.
