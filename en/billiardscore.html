BilliardScore - Front Office

BilliardScore is the front office part of our scoreboard solution: the software you use during matches to show scores and results to players and spectators.

Features

With BilliardScore, you get:

Match scoreboard

  • Simple scoreboard for training sessions and friendly matches
  • Scoreboard for individual competitions with match sheet
  • Clear display for players and spectators

Teams and networking

  • Scoreboard for team competitions with match sheet and summary
  • Multiple scoreboards can share data over the network
  • Useful for fixed club installations

Club branding

  • Support for custom advertising banners
  • Fits into existing screen setups
  • Ideal for clubs keeping their current workflow

Works Together with BilliardAdmin

BilliardScore works smoothly with BilliardAdmin (back office). Use BilliardAdmin to prepare competitions, member lists, and teams, then use BilliardScore to run the match and keep everything visible live.
