Privacy Policy – CaromStats

Last updated: 17 February 2026

CaromStats respects your privacy. The app is built to keep your billiard results and statistics safely on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information We Collect

CaromStats does not collect any personal information. All data you enter — such as match results, opponents, disciplines, and statistics — is stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database.

Cloud Backup (Optional)

CaromStats offers optional backup and restore functionality using:

  • iCloud Drive on iOS
  • Google Drive on Android

When you choose to use cloud backup:

  • Your data file is stored only in your own cloud account.
  • CaromStats does not access, read, or transmit this data.
  • No data is sent to any external server owned by the developer.

You may disable cloud backup at any time.

No Tracking, No Analytics

CaromStats does not use:

  • Tracking technologies
  • Analytics tools
  • Advertising frameworks
  • Third‑party data collection services

Your usage of the app is never monitored.

Data Security

All data remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up to your personal cloud storage. CaromStats does not have access to your cloud account or files.

Children’s Privacy

CaromStats does not collect personal data and is suitable for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and may also be shown within the app.
