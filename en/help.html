Our Software

We offer two tools that work nicely together as one complete solution:

BilliardScore

  • Front office for displaying scores during matches
  • Fits clubs with existing screens and PC setups
  • Useful when you still rely on live scoreboard display

BilliardAdmin

  • Back office for competition and member management
  • Helps you prepare matches, teams, and season data
  • Works together with BilliardScore in existing club workflows
