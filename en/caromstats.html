CaromStats - Mobile App

CaromStats is a modern mobile app for iOS and Android that helps you keep track of your billiard results and stats. A great fit if you like following your progress and improving your game over time.

Open in the App Store Ask a question
iOS + Android Built for modern phones and tablets.
Offline first Keep using your results even when you are offline.
Cloud backup Sync your data safely across devices when you want to.

✨ New in 2026!

CaromStats is the newest addition to our software lineup. Unlike BilliardScore and BilliardAdmin, it is a modern mobile app that is actively maintained and keeps growing with new features.

Platforms

  • iOS - iPhone and iPad (iOS 12.0 or later)
  • Android - Smartphones and tablets (Android 5.0 or later)

Main Features

📊 Advanced Statistics

  • Current average by discipline and by competition
  • Track your highest run
  • Won, lost, and drawn matches
  • Total points and innings played
  • Performance trends (rising, falling, stable)

📈 Visual Charts

  • Average evolution (line chart)
  • Highest run evolution (line chart)
  • Win/loss ratio (pie chart)
  • Per season or across all seasons

🎯 Smart Organization

  • Support for multiple disciplines
  • Season-based tracking with custom start date
  • Classification levels per discipline (min-max average)
  • Dashboard with discipline or competition view

☁️ Cloud Backup

  • Automatic backup to Google Cloud Firestore
  • Sign in with your Google account
  • Sync your data between devices
  • Smart conflict handling where the latest data wins

📥 CSV Import

  • Import historical results
  • Automatic date format detection
  • Validation and error handling
  • Detailed import feedback

🌐 Multilingual & Offline

  • Dutch, French, and English
  • Full functionality without an internet connection
  • Data stored locally on your device
  • Privacy-friendly

How It Differs from BilliardScore/Admin

CaromStats is built for individual players who want to keep an eye on their own performance. Compared with BilliardScore and BilliardAdmin:

  • ✅ Modern mobile app instead of desktop software
  • ✅ Actively maintained with regular updates
  • ✅ iOS and Android instead of Windows only
  • ✅ Cloud backup and synchronization
  • ✅ Personal use instead of club management
