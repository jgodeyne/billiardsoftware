v1.9.0 - 29/03/2026

  • Admin result entry now supports marking one player as absent per board.
  • When a player is absent, board victory is assigned automatically to the present opponent.
  • Boards with absence are excluded from personal player statistics and clearly indicated in match overviews.
  • In the standings, played matches are now shown directly after the team name.

v1.8.0 - 25/03/2026

  • Team detail now shows innings in player performance tables with improved layout.
  • Team and player identifiers were aligned across backend and frontend.
  • Captain section in admin was improved with compact status badges and clearer contact ordering.
  • Release process was hardened with automated release-note generation and workflow fixes.

v1.7.0 - 24/03/2026

  • Production workflow introduced automatic release-note creation.
  • Dashboard division/logo header was refined with updated logo assets.
  • Version and docker-compose management were aligned for more consistent releases.
