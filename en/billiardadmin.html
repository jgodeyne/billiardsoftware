BilliardAdmin - Back Office

BilliardAdmin is the back office part of our scoreboard solution: the software competition organizers use to prepare and manage competitions.

Download for Windows Watch videos
Back office Prepare competitions, member files, and teams in one place.
Windows desktop Fits existing club environments with fixed workstations.

Features

With BilliardAdmin, you can:

Members and structure

  • Create and manage member files
  • Keep a clear base for club administration
  • Useful when setting up a new season

Competition management

  • Configure individual competitions
  • Set up team competitions
  • Manage competition rules and settings

Works with BilliardScore

  • Prepare data for use in BilliardScore
  • Move smoothly from planning to live match display
  • Ideal for clubs with an existing match-day workflow

Works Together with BilliardScore

BilliardAdmin works smoothly with BilliardScore (front office). Prepare your competitions in BilliardAdmin, then use that data directly in BilliardScore during the match.
