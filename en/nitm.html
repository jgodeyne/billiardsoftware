NITM

NITM is the web application for the national three-cushion interclub competition, centralizing standings, teams, players, and match results.

Short description

NITM consolidates Triomphix competition data and makes it accessible for players, clubs, and team managers through a modern web experience.

  • Division standings and team details
  • Match and player-level results
  • Fast updates on competition status
